\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:prelim}

\paragraph{Heterogeneous graph and meta-path.} Following HAN \citep{wang2019heterogeneous}, a
heterogeneous graph $G = (V, E)$ comes with a node-type mapping $\phi \colon V \to \mathcal{A}$ and
an edge-type mapping $\psi \colon E \to \mathcal{R}$, where $|\mathcal{A}| + |\mathcal{R}| > 2$.
Ours has three node types: products (P), categories (C) and users (U). Its four relations are also
bought (P--P), leaf category (P--C), parent and child (C--C) and positive review (U--P). A meta-path
$A_1 \xrightarrow{R_1} A_2 \xrightarrow{R_2} \cdots \xrightarrow{R_l} A_{l+1}$ of $l$ hops
describes the composite relation $R_1 \circ R_2 \circ \cdots \circ R_l$. We write it by the initials
of its node types. Every path here starts and ends at a product: \code{PUP} links two products
reviewed by the same user, \code{PCP} two products in the same leaf category. A product's
neighbours along a path are the products that at least one instance of the path connects it to
\citep{wang2019heterogeneous}. Multiplying the adjacency matrices of the path's relations counts
these instances for each pair of products.

\paragraph{Task.} The positive reviews (edges between the users $\mathcal{U}$ and the products
$\mathcal{P}$) are split into training, validation and test edges (Section~\ref{sec:data}), and the
graph a model sees holds the training edges only. For a held-out edge $(u, p)$, a model ranks $p$
among the products that $u$ has no edge to. Every model ends with a vector $e_u$ per user and $h_p$
per product and ranks by $e_u^{\top} h_p$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:score}). Section~\ref{sec:setup} gives the
metrics.
